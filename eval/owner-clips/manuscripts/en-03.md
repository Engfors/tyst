# en-03 · The marathon

Category: en (Swedish-accented) · Language: en · Target: about 90 s
Tests: everyday vocabulary, Swedish place name in English (Stockholm).

---
I: I heard you ran the Stockholm Marathon this year. How did it go?
G: It went better than I expected, honestly. I finished in just under four hours, which was my goal. The first half felt easy, maybe too easy. Around kilometer thirty my legs started to complain, and the last part along the water was really tough.
I: How long did you train for it?
G: About five months. I followed a plan I found online, four runs a week. One long run on Sundays, a couple of shorter ones, and one session with intervals, which I hated every single time.
I: What kept you motivated?
G: I joined a running club near where I live. Having people waiting for you at seven in the morning, even when it's raining, makes it very hard to stay in bed. And they gave great advice about shoes and food and things like that.
I: Would you do it again?
G: Right after the race I said never again. Two days later I was already looking at races for next year. I think I'd like to try a trail race next time, something in the mountains, with fewer people and more nature.
